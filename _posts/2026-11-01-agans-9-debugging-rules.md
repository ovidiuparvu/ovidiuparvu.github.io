For more details read David J. Agans' book, [Debugging: The 9 Indispensable Rules for Finding Even the Most Elusive Software and Hardware Problems ](https://a.co/d/089D0w2q).

1. Understand the System
2. Make it Fail
3. Quit Thinking and Look
4. Divide and Conquer
5. Change One Thing at a Time
6. Keep an Audit Trail
7. Check the Plug
8. Get a Fresh View
9. If You Didn't Fix it, it Ain't Fixed
