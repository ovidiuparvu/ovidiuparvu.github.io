1. Share w/ your manager what and how you have been doing.
    - Complete agreed work. Be reliable in terms of delivery.
    - Give your manager a heads up as soon as possible when ETAs change unexpectedly.
2. Ask for feedback on your work.
3. Discuss about your professional goals and how to achieve them.
4. Ask about current and upcoming challenges for the team and for your manager, and how you can help.
5. Aim to have open and honest discussions.
