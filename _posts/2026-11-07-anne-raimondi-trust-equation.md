Anne Raimondi defines trust using the [Trust Equation](https://review.firstround.com/use-this-equation-to-determine-diagnose-and-repair-trust/):

$$
\text{Trust} = \frac{\text{Credibility} + \text{Reliability} + \text{Authenticity}}{\text{Perception of Self Interest}}
$$

This definition emphasizes that trust is built through demonstrating knowledge, keeping promises, and being genuine, while minimizing self-serving behaviors.
